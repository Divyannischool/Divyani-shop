Mandatory 85% Minimum Required

Scholars must maintain an aggregate attendance rate of at least 85% per academic semester to qualify for end-of-term examinations and honors standing. Unexcused consecutive absences exceeding 3 days trigger mandatory administrative review with student counselors.
